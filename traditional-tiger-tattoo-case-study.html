Composition analysis

What the finished tattoo shows.

Traditional tattooing depends on clarity. The tiger’s face, stripes, and outer silhouette need to remain understandable before the viewer studies smaller details.

The rounded shoulder changes how the design appears from the front, side, and back. A strong contour and controlled internal shapes allow the subject to stay intact as it wraps around the body.

Design challenges

How the composition solves the body area.

01

Silhouette

The outer shape communicates the tiger before individual stripes or color transitions are examined.

02

Placement

The design uses the shoulder’s curve instead of forcing a flat rectangular composition onto it.

03

Color separation

Dark outlines and limited color regions prevent adjacent forms from merging.

04

Distance test

The subject remains recognizable at normal viewing distance, a central strength of traditional design.
