About Josh

Art training and a shop in Oklahoma City.

Josh’s published biography describes graduating from Fort Lewis College in Colorado with a degree in art, then apprenticing at Monkey Wrench Tattoo under John Martin. It also identifies him as Oklahoma-born and describes his return to the state after working at that shop. Tornado Tattoo’s artist profile records his role as owner and artist since 2006. Today, the shop is at 3306 N MacArthur Blvd in Oklahoma City, where clients can contact Josh about custom tattoo projects.

Consultation process

What to bring when you contact Josh.

Give Josh a short description of the tattoo you want and the information needed to discuss its placement. A few clear references with notes about what you like are useful starting material. Include approximate dimensions, nearby tattoos, and whether you intend to add more work later. Share your budget and availability, then ask about the proposed design, estimate, appointment plan, and what needs to be agreed before booking. Use the checklist below to organize your request.

  • Describe the main subject and any personal meaning you want included. Identify specific people, characters, animals, objects, symbols, or wording, and rank the details that matter most.
  • Show the preferred body area and approximate height and width. Include photos from several angles, nearby tattoos, and the intended boundaries of a sleeve or larger project.
  • State whether you prefer full color, selected color accents, or black-and-gray. Bring examples of the palette or shading you like, including any colors you prefer to avoid.
  • Choose references that clearly show the subject, pose, drawing style, or texture you want. Explain what you like in each image and which parts you would change.
  • For a cover-up or rework, include current photos of the entire tattoo and surrounding area, measurements, and a description of what you want kept or changed.

Questions about booking Josh

Before you schedule.

What tattoo styles does Josh specialize in?+

Josh’s portfolio includes Japanese-inspired, horror, traditional, color, black-and-gray, ornamental, and large-scale work, along with cover-up and rework examples. The guides on this page give you subjects and planning questions to compare. For example, a horror portrait, an ornamental gemstone, and a samurai backpiece involve different reference material and placement discussions. Identify the examples closest to your idea and explain which features you like. Ask Josh about the proposed project, its size, and the treatment you prefer before choosing a design direction.

Can Josh design a custom tattoo from my idea?+

Tornado Tattoo offers custom tattooing. Bring the subject you want, references that show the details or style you prefer, and information about the placement. Explain which elements are essential and which you are open to changing. Include the exact spelling of any names, dates, or lettering. Use the consultation to discuss how the concept could fit the available area and ask about the design review process, estimate, and appointment arrangements. Confirm the proposed direction with Josh before committing to the tattoo.

Does Josh work on sleeves and backpieces?+

His portfolio includes arm compositions and backpieces, including the samurai example shown here and the owl and floral sleeve study linked below. For your own sleeve or backpiece, describe the complete area you eventually want covered, even if you plan to begin with one section. Show existing tattoos and rank your preferred subjects. Ask how the proposed sections would connect, what the appointment stages would involve, and how healing between visits would affect the schedule. Request an estimate for the design you agree on.

How do I request an appointment with Josh?+

Use the online booking link, call Tornado Tattoo at (405) 601-4445, or email tornadotattoookc@gmail.com. Say that you are requesting Josh and provide a short description, preferred placement, approximate size, and useful references. If the request involves existing tattoos, include current photos showing the full area. Ask whether to arrange a consultation before a tattoo appointment, and confirm the artist, date, estimate, and any booking requirements directly with the shop.
