Composition analysis

What the finished tattoo shows.

Repetition can unify a sleeve, but repeating the same size and intensity everywhere would remove hierarchy. The gemstone forms vary in position and relationship while sharing a recognizable palette and highlight language.

Dark ornamental structure separates bright blue sections and gives the sleeve a framework. The limited palette makes the concept feel intentional rather than like unrelated color pieces placed beside one another.

Design challenges

How the composition solves the body area.

01

Limited palette

Blue remains the dominant family while dark structure and lighter highlights create range.

02

Repeated language

Gemstone facets and ornamental shapes recur without becoming identical stamps.

03

Arm movement

The framework connects sections around the limb and avoids placing every focal point on one flat view.

04

Highlight control

Bright areas are reserved for gemstone reflections so the eye understands the material quickly.
