## Review request process

Ask only clients with a genuine experience. Do not offer discounts, free products, contest entries, or other incentives in exchange for reviews.

### In-person wording

“Thank you for trusting us with your tattoo. When you have a minute, an honest Google review about your experience helps people understand what it is like to work with the shop.”

### Text or email wording

“Thank you for choosing Tornado Tattoo. We appreciate the trust you placed in the shop. When you have time, we would be grateful for an honest Google review about your experience. Please share only what you are comfortable making public.”

Use the official Google review link or QR code generated from the Business Profile.

Official review guidance: https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122
Google can restrict profiles involved in fake or incentivized review activity: https://support.google.com/business/answer/14114287

## Review response templates

Personalize every response. Do not paste private appointment details, health information, or arguments about the client.

### Positive review

“Thank you, [First name]. We appreciate you trusting Tornado Tattoo with your [general project type]. It was great working with you, and we are glad the planning and final result matched what you were looking for.”

### Returning client

“Thank you for coming back, [First name]. Being trusted with another part of your collection means a lot to Josh and the shop. We appreciate the years of support.”

### Neutral or mixed review

“Thank you for the honest feedback, [First name]. We are glad [specific positive point] went well, and we take your concern about [general issue] seriously. Please contact the shop directly so we can understand what happened and discuss the appropriate next step.”

### Negative review

“Thank you for sharing your concern. We want every client to receive clear communication and professional service. We cannot discuss private appointment details publicly, but we would like to review the situation directly. Please call the shop at (405) 601-4445 or email tornadotattoookc@gmail.com.”

Do not report a review merely because it is negative. Report only content that violates Google policy.

Official review reporting guidance: https://support.google.com/business/answer/4596773
